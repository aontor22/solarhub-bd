FAQ

Common solar shopping questions

How do I choose the right inverter?

Match inverter capacity to the expected load, startup requirements and battery/system voltage. Product pages can be expanded with your exact engineering recommendations.

Can customers compare solar products?

Yes. Use the compare button on product cards, then open the comparison drawer from the header.

Are online payments already connected?

The checkout UI is ready for integration, but live payment processing requires your chosen payment provider credentials and production backend.

Can I add real brands and inventory?

Yes. Replace the demo catalog data in app.js or connect the UI to your own database/API.
